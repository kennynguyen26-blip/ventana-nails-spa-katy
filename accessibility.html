Accessibility Statement

Effective October 25, 2026

Ventana Nails & Spa Katy wants everyone to be able to use our website and enjoy our salon, including people with disabilities.

Our Commitment

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. Steps we take include:

  • Text descriptions (alt text) for meaningful images
  • Readable text sizes and color contrast
  • Pages that can be used with a keyboard and screen readers
  • A layout that works on phones, tablets, and computers, and respects reduced-motion settings

Ongoing Work

Accessibility is ongoing work. Some content, including our third-party booking system and the embedded Google Map, is provided by other companies and may not fully meet these standards.

Need Help or Found a Problem?

If you have trouble using any part of this website, we want to know, and we're happy to help you another way, including booking your appointment over the phone. Contact us by phone at (832) 639-3789, by email at ventana.nailsandspa@icloud.com, or in person at 22911 Clay Rd, Katy, TX 77449 (Galicia Retail Plaza). We try to respond within 2 business days.
